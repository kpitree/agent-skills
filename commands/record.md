Record `$ARGUMENTS` with `add_context`, attaching it to the metric it concerns when one
is named or clearly implied (find it with `search_metrics`). Record durable facts only:
an absence, a launch, an outage, a decision, a blocker, a change in the market. If the
text is a question rather than a fact, say so and answer it instead. Confirm what was
recorded, by name, never by UUID, and whether it was stored owners-only.
