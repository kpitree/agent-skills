PASS if the review has these parts: a headline naming the one or two findings that matter most (Revenue down 17.8% month to date led by the Leeds branch; online quote-to-sale conversion down since the pricing change); for each, the mechanism and the recorded why in a sentence; the objective finding that the "Grow revenue 10% this quarter" objective has delivered 70% of its work for 31% of its outcome, and that the online conversion objective is awaiting data rather than at zero; the portfolio verdicts (the pricing initiative is too early to judge, the referral programme is working, the Leeds second consultant has not started); what is in flight (the Leeds cover task, the initiative, the proposed actions not yet accepted); and one recommended action per problem with its owner, offering to record the decisions taken.
FAIL if it is a list of numbers without mechanism or recorded why, if it treats the awaiting-data objective as 0%, if it reports a self-reported impact as evidence, if it shows any UUID, or if it creates a task unasked.
